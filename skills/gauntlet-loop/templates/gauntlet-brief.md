# Gauntlet Brief

## Goal

What are we trying to make true?

## Result

What should exist, work, or be decided when we are done?

## Benchmark

What real reference, test, measurement, or outcome shows us what great looks like?

## Rules We Cannot Break

Facts, boundaries, exclusions, approvals, confidential information, and commercial constraints.

## Parts That Matter

The pieces that can be improved and judged separately.

## Depth

Quick, Standard, or Deep.

## Real-World Test

How will we try the complete result the way a real person would?

## Stop Conditions

What must be true before we call it done, and what would require the user's decision?
