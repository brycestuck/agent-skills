# Pathway patterns

Use these patterns to generate task-specific alternatives. They are examples of this skill's coaching method, not additional claims by Every.

## Increase the responsibility before polishing the prompt

| Current framing | More ambitious responsibility | Possible level and condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summarize these notes. | Prepare a usable recap, reconcile owners and dates against evidence, and put it into the working document. | 2 when working alongside the user; 3 or 4 when executing a bounded sequence. |
| Write this report again. | Run a defined process that gathers inputs, reconciles discrepancies, builds the report, and checks it. | 5 when the process and quality checks are repeatable. |
| Tell me what I should prepare for tomorrow. | Initiate a relevant briefing from calendar and supporting evidence, flagging what deserves attention. | 6 when an established trigger starts the work without a fresh request. |
| I assign research, writing, and checking to separate agents. | Give a coordinating agent the goal, allocation rules, integration standard, and escalation criteria. | 8 when the manager actually coordinates the agents. |
| Help me choose a launch plan. | Have orchestrated teams compete on different launch strategies using a grounded model and explicit scoring. | 9 when the teams and modeled competition actually exist. |
| Pick the strategy that won. | Repeat the competition with controlled variations and report robustness, failure cases, and unresolved assumptions. | 10 when repeated competitions test sensitivity and consistency. |

Levels are conditional on the actual arrangement. A prompt is a specification, not evidence that the required arrangement has been created.

## Workflow brief structure

Adapt this structure to the pathway rather than copying every field mechanically:

"Achieve [outcome] for [recipient or decision]. Use [known evidence and authorized tools]. Take responsibility for [specific work]. Complete [steps, roles, or event-triggered process]. Produce [usable deliverable] and check [observable standards]. Retain [human decision or already-required approval]. If [material uncertainty or exception], stop that part and explain what is needed. Report [result, evidence, and remaining limitations]."

For a Try now prompt, use available inputs and tools. For a Workflow design, distinguish the specification from any later setup or operation. Do not invent persistent monitoring, autonomous future work, or tool access.

## Three useful routes

Next useful step:
- Find a concrete handoff the user can improve now.
- Keep the output and check clear.
- Avoid adding a platform just to increase the level.

Larger redesign:
- Expand from an isolated request to a valuable process or broader outcome.
- Name what AI would own from start to finish.
- Include where context, review, triggers, or coordination will come from.

Frontier option:
- Stretch the outcome or operating design substantially.
- Explain why the additional role could matter.
- Name the conditions that would justify the investment.
- Use levels 9–10 only when competing strategies and meaningful uncertainty fit the actual decision.

## Examples of disciplined ambition

One annual thank-you note:
Keep conversational drafting as a reasonable option. A higher-level possibility could use existing event notes and work inside the user's draft, but honor a request to send it personally. Do not invent a multi-agent relationship-management system to justify a larger number.

An unbuilt chief-of-staff idea:
If the idea specifies a coordinating lead agent, specialists, and proactive briefings, discuss the intended orchestration plus assistant behavior. Start with one bounded briefing using actual inputs. Add repeatability, then triggers and coordination as the design earns trust. Do not equate the user's proposed architecture with demonstrated reliability.

One chatbot roleplaying several executives:
Recognize a useful deliberation exercise. Explain that multiple personas in one response do not establish separate agent teams. A pathway to level 9 requires real orchestration, grounded evaluation, and recorded competing strategies. A pathway to level 10 requires repeated competitions with meaningful variation.

A functioning advanced system with weak evidence:
Credit the described architecture while challenging model validity and decision quality. A more ambitious next move may be a better real-world experiment, representative data, or a new outcome. Do not propose another layer of agents merely to make the system sound advanced.

## Experiment contract

Choose one representative task. Compare the current and proposed approaches using:
- Quality of the final usable result.
- Human preparation, review, correction, and recovery.
- Missing evidence and unresolved exceptions.
- Whether the broader responsibility improved the actual outcome.

Suggest success criteria appropriate to the task. Mark any numeric threshold as a proposal. Expand only if the evidence supports it; retain the parts that work even if the target level changes.
